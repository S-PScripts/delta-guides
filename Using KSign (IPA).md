# Using KSign (IPA)
## Downloading the IPA:
- Go to https://deltaexploits.dev or https://deltaexploits.gg
- Download the Delta IPA

## Installing the Delta IPA:
- Open Ksign. If it's showing "unable to verify" or crashes upon opening, you need another Ksign. Delete the one you already have.
- Press "Library", then press the + at the top right.
- Press Import From Files, then find the Delta IPA you downloaded and press it.
- Once Delta appears, press it and press "Sign and Install". Then, press Start Signing
- Wait for it to install. When prompted, press Install.
