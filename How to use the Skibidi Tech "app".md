# How to use the Skibidi Tech "app" (bookmark)
Note: Skibidi Tech is probably safe; it's an unofficial partner of Delta, and he's the one to make direct install.

1. Turn off your VPN if you have one and disable other DNS if you have them. After that, go to https://skibiditech.co
2. Press Get Started. If you're redirected to an advertisement, go back to the previous tab/press back.
3. Allow the configuration profile.
4. Press Close.
5. Go to Settings.
6. Go to General -> VPN and Device Management.
7. Press Install. Enter your password if necessary, then press Install again. Press it once again.
8. Open the Skibidi Tech bookmark.
9. You can now choose to install ESign, Scarlet, KSign, (TrollStore), or Feather. You should choose KSign.
10. Press KSign/app of your choosing, then press the download button next to the one at the top. If you're redirected to an ad, just close it and press again.
11. Press Install, then wait on your home screen.
12. Press on KSign/the signer app; if you get an integrity error, delete it and install another certificate. Otherwise, go to Settings. Go to VPN and Device Management again, trust the certificate, and restart your device if necessary.
13. Download the Delta IPA from https://deltaexploits.dev.
