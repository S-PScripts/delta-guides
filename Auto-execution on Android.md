## Auto-execution on Android (2026)
> In Delta's settings (cog icon), make sure "Auto Execute" is enabled!
1. Open a game in Delta. If you have not already, complete the keysystem.
2. Click the icon of the house in the Delta GUI.
3. Click Upload, then enter a title for the script.
4. Enter the script.
5. Open the ZArchiver app on your device. If you do not have it installed, go to the Play Store to get it.
6. Go to the path: storage/emulated/0
7. Press on the folder called "Delta", then press on the Scripts folder.
8. Hold on the script you uploaded, then press Copy.
9. Swipe back, press on the Autoexecute folder, then press the clipboard icon.
10. You are done!
