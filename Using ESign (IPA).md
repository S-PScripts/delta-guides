# Using ESign (IPA)

## Old ESign
- Click Ok
- Click Agree
- Click Agree again
  Click ...
- Click Import
- Find the Delta IPA (download at https://deltaexploits.dev) and click it
- Click Import
- Once the IPA is imported and it says "imported successfully", click it and click Import App Library (DO NOT CLICK INSTALL)
- Go to Apps
- Click Signature
- Click Signature again
- Wait until it finishes signing, then click Install
- Click Install again, then wait
- Delta should be on your home screen!

## New ESign
> This application is also called "Ysign".
1. Open ESign.
2. Allow (or Don't Allow) notifications.
3. Click Next for the User Service Agreement, then click Agree.
4. Click the ... button at the top right, then click Import from Files.
5. Click on the Delta IPA you downloaded (get it at https://deltaexploits.dev if you don't have it already), then click Open.
6. Once it's imported, press the IPA, then click Import to App Library.
7. Wait for the IPA to unzip. Afterwards, click Confirm. Click Confirm again.
8. Go to Apps and click on Delta.
9. Click Sign, then click Start Signing.
10. Wait for it to sign. When prompted to install Delta, do so
> If you aren't automatically prompted, you may need to click Go to Install, then click Signed, then click the Install button next to Delta.
11. Click Confirm. On your home screen, Delta should be there!
> If you aren't getting any install prompts, go to ESign's Settings and press IPA Install Server. Change it to one of the Backup Servers and try to install again.

> IF YOU GET A CODERESOURCES ERROR, PRESS AND EXTRACT THE IPA ONCE IT IS INSIDE ESIGN. AFTERWARDS, SIGN THE IPA INSIDE THE PAYLOAD FOLDER THAT IS CREATED.
