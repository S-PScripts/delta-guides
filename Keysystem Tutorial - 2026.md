# Keysystem Tutorial - 2026
1. open delta

2. join a roblox game; joining a baseplate game is preferred

3. wait until delta loads

4. press receive key. on ios, you'll get shown two blue buttons; press them both and wait 30 seconds. once done, press click to continue
> if you get a failed to clear cache error: https://discord.com/channels/1279547031945220147/1414621580775919646

5. platorelay should open; press continue (if platorelay didn't open, exit out of delta, open your browser, and paste the link that was copied to your clipboard)
> if redirected to a popup ad, go back to the platorelay tab and press continue again
> if you see a connection error or nothing loads afterwards, read the troubleshooting section

6. press the 1st task on lootlabs then go back to the lootlabs page after 5 seconds (don't actually do the task). repeat for all tasks.
> if you see a connection error or nothing loads afterwards, read the troubleshooting section

7. wait until the task countdowns hit 0. if a task doesn't have a number after you click on it, or if it hits 0 but doesn't complete, reload the page
> if it still doesn't work after many reloads, read the troubleshooting section

8. once all the tasks complete successfully, go to the platorelay page and press "receive key"
> if redirected to a popup ad, go back to the platorelay tab and press receive key again

9. copy the key, open delta again, and paste it in. now you can enjoy delta for 24 hours before needing to redo the keysys again

troubleshooting:
- enable mobile/cellular data (with wifi disabled)
- enable wifi (with mobile/cellular data disabled)
- use another browser (safari, chrome, brave, edge, firefox, opera, duckduckgo)
- use desktop mode for your browser
- use incognito mode
- use a vpn (protonvpn, 1.1.1.1 warp)
- use linkvertise (if you're on iOS)
- use https://browser.lol
- send the keysystem link to another device, do it there
