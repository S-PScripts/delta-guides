# Failed to cache link
Install a VPN from the App/Play Store, such as Proton VPN or 1.1.1.1/Cloudflare WARP
If that does not work, you may need to clear your cache:

## For ColorOS:
1. Hold the Roblox icon in App Library
2. Press App info
3. Press Storage usage
4. Press Clear cache

## For OneUI:
1. Hold the Roblox icon in App Library
2. Press the i button on the right
3. Press Storage
4. Press Clear cache

Other skins of Android may differ slightly.
